# Company Insights Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

Company Insights is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze company employees, sales, profit, salary, performance, and business trends.

The dashboard provides a simple and visual way to understand company performance and employee-related insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee distribution across departments, roles, cities, and gender.
- Track total sales and profit.
- Compare sales across different regions and categories.
- Analyze average salary by department.
- Understand employee performance.
- Identify sales trends over time.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel / CSV datasets

## 📁 Dataset

The project uses three main datasets:

- `employees` – Employee information such as department, role, city, salary, gender, experience, and performance.
- `departments` – Department budget, headcount, and manager information.
- `sales` – Sales, profit, category, region, customer, date, and discount information.

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards
- Total Employees
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Average Salary
- Average Performance

### Employee Analysis
- Employee Distribution by Gender
- Employee Count by Role
- Employee Count by City
- Average Salary by Department
- Average Performance by Department

### Sales Analysis
- Total Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales Trend Over Time

### Filters
Interactive filters are provided for:
- Department
- City
- Date

## 📈 Key Insights

The dashboard allows users to interactively explore company data and understand how employee and business metrics change based on selected filters.

## 📷 Dashboard

The final Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of company performance and workforce insights.
